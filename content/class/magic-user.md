> A **Magic User** studies the strange forces awakened by abundant Zohm, guiding them through knowledge, discipline and dangerous practice.

A **Magic User** may be a secretive conjurer, feared spirit-worker or eccentric scholar burdened with peculiar tools. Their practices can call lightning, influence perception, stir unseen presences or hasten the closing of wounds.

Such feats are powerful but never entirely predictable. Channelled Zohm follows the meaning held by Forms and Names, and even an expert may receive more than intended. No working is attempted lightly. Like any traveller of the Wastes, an adept still understands the value of a sharp blade and steady hand.
