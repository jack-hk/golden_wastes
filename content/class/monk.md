> A **Monk** is a pilgrim and martial artist who seeks harmony between body, mind and [True Life](/lore/true-life/).

A **Monk** may be a peacekeeping wanderer, reclusive mystic or solitary guardian. Raised within an order, they often set aside inheritance and excess possessions to pursue discipline, understanding and balance.

Unarmed and lightly equipped, monks are easily underestimated. A lifetime of training turns movement, breath and awareness into weapons of remarkable precision. Their teachings often consider the balance of [Order and Chaos](/lore/true-life/#order-and-chaos), seeking neither rigid stillness nor uncontrolled impulse. Through discipline, they perform feats others consider impossible, standing firm as stone and yielding as wind.
