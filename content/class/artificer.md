> An **Artificer** is an alchemist, forge master and powder savant who channels Zohm through carefully made devices.

An **Artificer** may be a reclusive tinkerer, a broad-shouldered gadgeteer, a fume-addled apothecary or a proud steamsmith. Their craft combines metalwork, alchemy and a dangerous understanding of how material can guide Zohm.

Hammer in hand and fire at their belt, artificers travel in search of rare substances, forgotten techniques and chances to test their creations. Many folk regard them as reckless meddlers in unstable forces, perhaps with reason. Yet every kingdom fades, while the right discovery may outlive them all.
