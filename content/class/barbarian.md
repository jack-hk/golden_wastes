> A **Barbarian** is a hardened warrior shaped by the mountains, sprawling deserts, salt flats or other unforgiving lands.

A **Barbarian** may be a fierce raider, a wandering champion or the sworn protector of a remote people. Long years among harsh terrain develop formidable endurance, quick instincts and a ferocity rarely matched by those raised behind settled walls.

Barbarians are skilled with many weapons, but favour armour that leaves them free to climb, run and strike without restraint. Their traditions are often carried through memory and spoken lore rather than writing. Many distrust scholars who channel Zohm, believing discipline, keen senses and tested steel to be more dependable.
