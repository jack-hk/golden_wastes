> A **Fighter** is a veteran warrior trained to master weapons, armour and the changing demands of battle.

A **Fighter** may be a mail-clad knight, an agile spearman or a sharp-eyed bowman. Unlike warriors devoted to a single tradition, fighters practise with many armaments and can change their stance, equipment and tactics to meet the foe before them.

Fighters are often the most heavily armed and armoured members of an adventuring company. They belong in the press of battle, where judgement matters as much as strength. Against raiders, beasts or stranger enemies, they rely upon discipline, sound steel and a lifetime spent learning how to survive the next blow.
