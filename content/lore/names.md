> **Names** are words, titles or phrases given by intelligent beings with [True Life](/lore/true-life/).

A **Name** allows many people to recognise the same person, object, place, event or idea. Each time a Name is spoken, remembered, known or thought about, the thing it identifies attracts more [Zohm](/lore/zohm/).

Widely recognised Names attract more Zohm than forgotten or little-known Names. If enough Zohm gathers within something, it may develop unpredictable powers.

## Fell Names

**Fell Names** are evil Names shaped by cruelty, corruption and other dark acts. Across generations, civilisations abused the power of Naming granted by [Oru](/lore/oru/), using Fell Names to conquer and control others.

To weaken these Names, the planet released enormous amounts of Zohm, causing the [Great Pulses](/lore/great-pulse/). When the flow later receded, it damaged memory and recognition throughout the world. Entire generations of knowledge were lost. This event became known as the [Memory Plague](/lore/memory-plague/).
