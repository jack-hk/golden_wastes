> A **[Fell Name](/lore/fell-names/)** is a [Name](/lore/names/) shaped by deliberate malice, cruelty or the desire to inflict unjust suffering. Most peoples recognise such Names as inherently evil and refuse to cultivate them.

A **Fell Name** influences a [Form](/lore/zohm/#form) through malicious meanings that true Life has knowingly attached to it.

Some emerge slowly around feared rulers, places or objects. Others are created deliberately so that their reputation will spread, inspire dread or serve an evil purpose.

## Moral Meaning

[True Life](/lore/life/) does not require perfect knowledge to recognise malicious intent.

Cultures may disagree over laws, rulers and acts of war, but a Name deliberately created to celebrate cruelty, degrade others or turn suffering into a source of power usually reveals its nature clearly. Such Names are shunned by most peoples as evil and dangerous.

The strongest Fell Names leave little room for honest doubt. Their intent is recognised through the deliberate malice upon which their meaning was built.

## Disputed Names

Some Names exist near the boundary of what might be considered Fell.

A grim title may have arisen from necessity rather than malice, while a feared place or object may still serve a just or protective purpose. Different peoples may therefore understand its meaning in different ways.

Such uncertainty is uncommon among powerful Fell Names. Names deliberately shaped through evil intent are recognised and condemned by the great majority of cultures.

## Wounds upon the World

![](/uploads/tablet-part-2.png)

{width="280" align="right" mobileMinWidth="200" mobileMaxWidth="280" caption="The strongest Fell Names leave little room for honest doubt."}

Fell Names influence how a Form’s [Zohm](/lore/zohm/) behaves.

As their recognition spreads, the meanings they carry settle more deeply into the world. Places begin to reflect old fears, objects express the purposes for which they are remembered and malicious ideas may endure long after their creators are gone.

The old civilisations cultivated Fell Names until these wounds became too great for the world to bear. Later peoples ignored their warnings and repeated the same mistake.

Both [Great Zohm Pulses](/lore/first-great-pulse/) followed ages in which Fell Names had grown powerful across much of the world.

## The World’s Healing

The Great Pulses were the world’s attempts to undo the damage carried by Fell Names.

Abundant Zohm awakened those Names and forced their meanings into visible Form, but the resulting transformations brought ruin far beyond their intended targets.

[Memory Plagues](/lore/memory-plagues/) continued this healing by weakening Names through forgetting. Fell Names faded alongside languages, discoveries and histories that had caused no harm.

The world rejects what has wounded it, but its healing makes no distinction between what deserves to be lost and what deserves to remain.
