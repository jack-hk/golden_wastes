Welcome to the **[Golden Wastes](/article/the-wastes/)**, a post-apocalyptic fantasy setting and **[project](/article/the-project/)** created for [TTRPG play](/article/how-to-play/) and world-building.

The project explores a brutally harsh region between one and three centuries after the global apocalypse known as the **[Second Great Pulse](/article/second-great-pulse/)**.

Scorching salt flats, towering teeth-like formations and all kinds of riches stretch as far as the eye can see, while far below lies a colossal network of caverns known simply as **[The Beneath](/location/the-beneath/)**.
