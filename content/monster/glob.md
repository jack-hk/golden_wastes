> The **Glob** is a tough, ooze-like creature whose movements seem to defy natural laws.

Despite its heavy, semi-liquid body, it can travel at surprising speed by shifting its weight and rolling across the ground. When attacking, a **Glob** launches itself towards its target before suddenly hardening into a dense projectile capable of crushing armour, breaking bone and knocking a grown person from their feet.

Most Globs are roughly a quarter of a human’s size, though rare specimens have been reported to reach twelve feet in height and width.

## Appearance

A Glob is a rounded mass of silver, semi-transparent fluid with a reflective metallic sheen. Its inner organs can be seen suspended within its body, drifting and twisting as it moves. Globs may appear in other colours, but silver is by far the most common.

When threatened, a Glob can transform into a solid, opaque form resembling polished metal. In this state, its soft body becomes incredibly dense and difficult to damage.

## Behaviour

Globs are most often found in flat, open terrain, where they can build speed without obstacles, though some also inhabit low hills and gently sloping ground. They prefer to hunt at night, rolling across the landscape in search of animals, plants and almost anything else they can digest.

Globs are omnivorous. They consume food by pulling it into their gel-like body, where it remains suspended while powerful fluids slowly dissolve and digest it.

Small portions of a Glob’s slime can detach from the main body without harming the creature, provided no vital organs are carried away with them. These loose pieces may continue moving for a short time and can later be reabsorbed by the original Glob.

## Alchemy

The acidic gel within a Glob is a common and highly valued alchemical ingredient. Its exceptional potency makes it useful in a wide variety of specialised mixtures, though harvesting and storing it safely requires suitable equipment and considerable care.
