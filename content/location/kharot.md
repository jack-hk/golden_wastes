> **The Kha'rot Ashlands** are a hostile expanse within the Gharob region, covered in pale, salty ash and scattered remains.

Thick ash-fog often reduces visibility, while violent **Bonestorms** sweep across the land without warning. Their abrasive winds can strip exposed skin and make open travel extremely dangerous.

Bonestorms also draw [Bonestalkers](/monster/bonestalker/) and other undead servants of Master Shaal. These creatures follow in the storms' wake, gathering remains to strengthen his forces.

Few travellers cross Kha'rot without protection, preparation and a clear route. Between poor visibility, unstable weather and the creatures that roam behind the storms, the Ashlands are considered one of Gharob's most dangerous regions.
