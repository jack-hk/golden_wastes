> **Snaggleteeth** is a hot, arid region marked by enormous black stone spires rising from cracked ground.

Between **Snaggleteeth’s** spires lie countless craters, ranging from broad pits to vast chasms as wide as mountain bases. Some are said to descend all the way into [the Beneath](/location/the-beneath/).

Days are brutally hot beneath the exposed sun, while nights become bitterly cold. The rocky terrain is fractured and difficult to cross.

[Spire](/location/spire/) is one of the region’s best-known settlements. Once a much larger town before the [Second Great Pulse](/lore/great-pulse/#the-second-great-pulse), it now survives as a rare pocket of civilisation in one of Gharob’s most hostile regions.
