> **The Beneath** is a colossal network of caverns far below the [Golden Wastes](/location/the-wastes/).

Once only a myth, **The Beneath** became real during the [First Great Pulse](/lore/great-pulse/)

Its depths lie closer to the world’s core, where [Zohm](/lore/zohm/) originates. This makes Zohm far more abundant than on the surface. Channeling it is easier, and objects often possess greater power.

{{< textbox title="Supernatural" align="right" style="plain" icon="/SVG/open-book.svg">}}  
The abundance of **Zohm** makes the Beneath extremely dangerous. Strange effects, altered creatures and unstable regions are common.  
{{< /textbox >}}

The Beneath is divided into many layers and sub-layers, each with its own environments, hazards and paths deeper underground.
